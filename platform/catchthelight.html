🌞 Welcome to Catch the Light!

Help your plant perform photosynthesis by catching light photons ☀️ and water droplets 💧 while avoiding pollutants ⚫.

Every time you gather enough energy, your plant releases oxygen (O₂) — just like in the light-dependent reactions that happen inside chloroplasts.

🎯 Your Goals

  • ☀️ Catch light → creates ATP & NADPH (energy)
  • 💧 Catch water → releases oxygen (O₂)
  • ⚫ Avoid pollutants → they drain energy
  • 🌱 Try to release as much O₂ as possible before time runs out!

🧠 Educational Value: This game models the light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis — showing how plants convert sunlight and water into energy and oxygen.
